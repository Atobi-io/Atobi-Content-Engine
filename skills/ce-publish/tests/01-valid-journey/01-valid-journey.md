# Fresh Foam X 1080v15: what changed and how to sell it

## Who walks in asking for it

A runner who already owns a 1080 and wants the next one. They know the shoe.
They want to know what is different.

![Side profile of the 1080v15](side-profile.jpg)
*The v15 midsole is taller at the heel.*

### Knowledge check

**Q:** A customer says the 1080 feels too soft. What do you say?
- [ ] Suggest a firmer model right away
- [x] Explain that Fresh Foam X is tuned for cushioning and ask about their running
- [ ] Offer a half size down
> Feedback: Soft is the design intent. Reassure first, then qualify.

## What changed from the v14

Three things: more foam under the heel, a new knit upper, and a wider toe box
in the standard width.

- Heel stack up 2 mm
- Hypoknit upper replaces engineered mesh
- Standard width now fits like the old wide

## Closing the fitting

Get them to run ten steps on the shop floor before you talk price.
